How a lesson works

Story, game, quiz, reward

Each lesson takes about ten minutes and follows the same rhythm, so children always know what comes next.

  1. 📖
    StoryThree short cards with a read-aloud button.
  2. 🎮
    GameCount coins, run a stall, plan a budget, spot a scam.
  3. ✅
    QuizFive questions drawn from a bank of twelve, with gentle hints.
  4. ⭐
    RewardStars, XP, levels, streaks and badges.

Questions

Good to know

Is it free?

This is a prototype. Pricing for families and schools is still to be decided.

Can my child trade shares in the app?

No. The practice market is a game with made-up companies and made-up years. No real money moves and nothing is recommended.

Do children need an account?

They pick a nickname, and a parent or guardian confirms they are with the child. Nothing else is collected.

Which languages?

English today. More languages are planned.
