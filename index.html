How a lesson works

Story, game, quiz, reward

Every lesson takes about ten minutes and follows the same rhythm, so children always know what comes next.

  1. 📖StoryThree short cards, with a read-aloud button.
  2. 🎮GameCount coins, run a stall, plan a budget, spot a scam.
  3. ✅QuizFive questions drawn from a bank of twelve. Gentle hints, no penalties.
  4. ⭐RewardStars, XP, levels, streaks and badges.

For parents and schools

Built to be safe by design

  • Practice money onlyNo real trading, payments or products. The practice market uses made-up companies.
  • No advice, no tipsLessons explain ideas such as risk, inflation and loans. They never say what to buy.
  • Light on dataA nickname and progress. No chat, no ads, no location.
  • Parent cornerLessons done, first-try accuracy, minutes learned and a conversation starter for every lesson.
  • Light and dark themesComfortable by day and at bedtime, with an auto setting that follows the device.
  • Made for IndiaRupees, UPI, Vishu Kaineettam, SIPs, FDs and the national cybercrime helpline 1930.

Questions

Good to know

Is it free?

This is a prototype. Pricing for families and schools is still to be decided.

Can my child trade shares in the app?

No. The practice market is a game with made-up companies and made-up years. No real money moves and nothing is recommended.

Do children need an account?

They pick a nickname, and a parent or guardian confirms they are with the child. Nothing else is collected.

Which languages?

English today. More languages are planned.

Ready to play?
